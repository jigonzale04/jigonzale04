CASE BRIEFINGYour assignment

QE

You are a Quality Engineering intern at Riverton Motors. Riverton is qualifying a new machine shop to supply the bore diameter on its front brake rotor. Before any parts ship to the assembly line, you have to prove — with real measurement data — that the vendor's process can hold the print's tolerance.

Each inspection batch you pull from the shop floor gives you 10 rotor measurements. For every batch, you'll compute the mean, the standard deviation, and then the two numbers Riverton's Supplier Quality team actually cares about: Cp (could this process ever be capable, if centered?) and Cpk (is it capable right now, wherever the mean happens to sit?).

You'll repeat this for three separate batches — Try 1, Try 2, Try 3 — so you can see how the verdict shifts if the vendor's process drifts or tightens between shipments.

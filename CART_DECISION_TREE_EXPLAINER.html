⚠️ Without pruning, a CART tree perfectly memorises training data (100% train accuracy) but overfits. Cost-complexity pruning adds penalty α per leaf — increasing α progressively collapses the tree toward its root.
✅ The optimal α is found via cross-validation. In scikit-learn: DecisionTreeClassifier(ccp_alpha=α). The sweet spot balances training accuracy with model complexity.

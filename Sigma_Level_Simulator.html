Sigma level means
how many process standard deviations fit from the process mean to the nearest specification limit.

Olivia Carter is the quality manager at Liberty Springs Bottling. Her firm buys refill cartridges that must be filled to a target of 100 grams. Customers will accept cartridges only if the fill weight stays between the Lower Specification Limit (LSL = 90) and the Upper Specification Limit (USL = 110).

Your job: compare providers and decide which one is safest for Liberty Springs. Each provider has a different process standard deviation, so each one achieves a different sigma level relative to the same customer specifications.

Core idea

Sigma level = distance from the process mean to the nearest specification limit ÷ process standard deviation

In this case: the process is centered at 100, and the nearest specification limit is 10 units away.

Nearest spec distance = min(USL − μ, μ − LSL) = min(110 − 100, 100 − 90) = 10

Therefore:

Sigma level = 10 ÷ σ
